## 1. Research Scope
Research the following maritime logistic categories (do not assume any named leader is "best" — discover alternatives):

1. Terminal Operating Systems (TOS) — enterprise + mid-market
2. Yard / Container Management Systems (YMS)
3. Port Community Systems (PCS)
4. Container tracking & IoT
5. Gate / appointment management
6. Resource & equipment management
7. Reporting & analytics in terminal operations
8. SOA/microservice patterns applied in the maritime industry
